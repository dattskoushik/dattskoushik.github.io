About Me

Data Engineer specializing in idempotent pipelines and high-reliability infrastructure.

My professional focus is on architecting and maintaining high-throughput data pipelines using Python and Spark. I build systems that survive the initial commit—pipelines that are idempotent, robust, and designed to fail gracefully rather than silently dropping data.

I thrive on solving data engineering problems that require deep technical understanding. Whether it's optimizing PostgreSQL queries to reduce latency, managing schema evolution in distributed environments, or building real-time streaming architectures with Kafka, I prioritize correctness and data quality above all else.

Currently, I am targeting Data Engineering roles where I can leverage my expertise in ETL/ELT, dimensional modeling, and distributed systems to build scalable analytics platforms. I enjoy environments that value maintainability and simplicity over unnecessary complexity.
